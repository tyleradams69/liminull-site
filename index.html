How it operates

AI is not the product. The operating layer is.

The point is not to replace the owner, dispatcher, estimator, or sales rep. The point is to remove avoidable delay, make follow-up visible, and give the human operator better drafts, cleaner queues, and fewer dropped balls.

01
Audit the leaks

Map where leads die: unclear offers, slow replies, missed calls, weak proof, dead CRM stages.

02
Build the minimum useful system

Pages, automations, CRM rules, approval gates, and dashboard views that serve the actual workflow.

03
Launch with restraints

Human approval where it matters. Conservative defaults. Clear logs. No reckless automation.

04
Operate and tighten

Improve routing, copy, response quality, proof, and local visibility as real behavior comes in.
